## Definition and coverage findings

- GST is domestic collection, not state post-settlement receipts. Cess appears in the source total through FY2024–25 and is absent from subsequent component layouts. Both the published total and a separately labelled CGST+SGST+IGST outcome are exported.
- E-Way Bill files start July 2018 and end August 2026. Inward/outward/intra-state categories remain separate.
- CPI bases remain separate. The 2024 back-series response contains All India only. The old-base state series has missing April–May 2020 observations. No synthetic or interpolated substitutes were added.
- Stable name/code mappings were validated against source metadata. J&K transition months, unsupported pre-formation Ladakh data and unresolved legacy merged-territory definitions are quarantined. See the mapping CSV for every source name/code/status and observed date range.
- Reference-month intersection: **2,867 state-months** with CPI base 2012 and **700 state-months** with CPI base 2024. Historical geography IDs are not a claim that all present-day states have a complete common history. Exact per-state counts and internal gaps appear in the coverage JSON.

## Availability limitation

The archive contains current downloaded vintages, not all original monthly releases. Publication dates for those exact historical values have not been established. Strict joins therefore produce **zero complete historical rows**; the code will not backdate revised values. A verified source-hash/month release registry is supported for original archived vintages.

The explicitly labelled latest-vintage/45-day-assumed-lag mode produces **2,858 complete rows** using CPI base 2012 and **595** using base 2024. These are sensitivity-analysis inputs, not evidence of real-time forecast performance. Assumed lags do not remove revision bias. The client loader requires explicit opt-in to use them.

## Validation performed

- 23 offline tests passed: period/column parsing, official workbook layouts, incomplete pagination, numerical validation, three official PDF layouts, geography changes, availability cutoffs, revised vintages, CPI-base separation and synthetic-input refusal.
- All 110 monthly GST workbook totals reconciled against their component/state rows at the configured tolerance.
- 70 state-month PDF/workbook comparisons (35 matched states each for September and December 2023) agreed within INR 0.51 crore rounding tolerance. The merged territory was omitted from this direct-name comparison rather than combined without evidence.
- September and December 2023 multi-page PDFs and September 2026 single-page PDF each yielded 36 current-month state/UT rows plus two non-client jurisdictions. Long-name cell artifacts were visually inspected and repaired only by exact observed patterns.
- PDF figures remain a separate verification stream. The December 2023 PDF lacks the explicit imports footnote in its extracted text and is marked definition-unverified rather than assigned a presumed scope.

## Remaining evidence required

Original-release vintage histories are needed for strict historical backtesting. Post-reorganisation statistical boundaries need source-specific documentation before quarantined records can enter a model. A comparable state-level CPI backcast cannot be inferred from an All-India series. Further PDF layouts require explicit adapters and reviewed fixtures. Model experiments, forecast gains and privacy guarantees were not part of this data integration.
